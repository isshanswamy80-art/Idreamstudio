- 00_full_cinematic_poster.png — Home hero/background
- 01_hero_cinematic_couple.png — Home visual gallery / storytelling
- 02_wedding_cinematic.png — Home + Services wedding visual

- 05_cinematic_castle_background.png — Home + Local Ads cinematic visual
