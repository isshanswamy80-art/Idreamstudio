I DREAM STUDIO WEBSITE

Open index.html directly, or serve this folder with any static web server.

Pages
- index.html — Home
- about.html — Director profile
- services.html — Personal cinematic films
- local-ads.html — Local business commercials and CAD pricing
- contact.html — Contact and WhatsApp

Shared files
- style.css — complete responsive cinematic/editorial visual system
- script.js — accessible mobile navigation, header state, restrained reveal motion and optional YouTube embeds
- assets/ — original supplied artwork
- assets/web/ — smaller web-ready JPEG derivatives used by the pages
- robots.txt and sitemap.xml — search-engine discovery

Content notes
- Pricing, service details, geography and contact details come from the supplied business material.
- The Local Ads demo section intentionally contains no invented client work. To add a real YouTube film, place its video ID in the relevant data-video-id attribute in local-ads.html. The placeholder will be replaced automatically with a privacy-enhanced embed.
- YouTube, Instagram and Facebook are listed without links because profile URLs were not supplied.
