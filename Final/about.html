I Dream Studio is built on approximately 35 years in the film industry. The founder’s experience includes work as an Assistant Director and independent director, time in Mumbai’s film industry, and festival-recognized work.

That background now meets modern AI video tools. Every project is approached with the fundamentals that make a film feel like a film: story structure, considered shot composition, emotional pacing and filmmaking instinct.

Based in Toronto, Ontario, I Dream Studio serves individuals and businesses across North America and worldwide.
